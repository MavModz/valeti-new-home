Valeti builds custom homes across Melbourne and Victoria. We specialise in single and double storey homes, farmhouses, knockdown rebuilds, multi-unit developments, and display homes. Every project is tailored to your block, budget and lifestyle.

Timelines vary by design, size and site. A typical single storey may take around 6–12 months from contract to handover; double storey and more complex designs can be longer. We give you a clear timeline and keep you updated at every stage.

Yes. Valeti has extensive experience in knockdown rebuilds. We manage demolition, design and build on your existing block so you can stay in the area you love with a brand-new home.

Yes. We have display homes you can walk through to see quality, finishes and layout ideas. Contact us to book a visit or check our website for locations and opening times.

Price depends on your design, inclusions, site conditions and any upgrades. We provide transparent quotes and work with you to stay within budget while meeting your needs.

We build across Melbourne and Victoria, including Cranbourne and the south-eastern suburbs. Get in touch to confirm we build in your area.

Yes. We work from our range of designs, your architect’s plans, or a mix of both. We can adapt layouts, facades and inclusions to suit your block and preferences.

Yes. Valeti undertakes multi-unit and townhouse projects for investors and owner-builders. We can guide you through design, approvals and construction.

Get in touch for a free consultation. We’ll discuss your block, budget and ideas, then arrange a site assessment and guide you through design and quote options.

Valeti has over 24 years of experience in the industry. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern standards to deliver quality custom homes.

We build to Australian standards and provide appropriate structural and defect warranties. Exact terms are outlined in your contract. We stand behind our work and support you after handover.

Contact us by phone on 03 8804 5757 or email Info@valeti.com.au. Share your block details and what you’re looking for, and we’ll arrange a consultation and provide a clear, detailed quote.

We focus on designing and building your home. If you already have land, we can assess it and design to suit. We can also point you to resources if you’re still looking for a block.

We’re a family business with 24+ years of experience, a focus on custom design rather than one-size-fits-all, and a commitment to clear communication and quality. We build single and double storey, farmhouses, knockdown rebuilds and multi-unit projects under one roof.
