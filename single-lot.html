Single Lot House Designs

At Valeti, our Single Lot House Designs are tailored to suit a wide range of block sizes and lifestyles. Whether you have a compact lot or a generous parcel of land, we offer flexible floor plans that maximize space, light, and livability. You can filter our designs by lot width and depth, then refine by storey, bedrooms, bathrooms, ensuite, living areas, car spaces, and facade style to find the perfect match for your block and needs.

Every design is backed by our experience in residential construction and our commitment to quality and clear communication. Browse the options below, then explore our full range of floor plans or visit one of our display homes to see the quality in person.

Lot Sizes We Cater For

Our single lot designs are available across the following dimensions so you can find a plan that fits your block.

Lot width

10 m · 12.5 m · 14 m · 16 m · 32 m

Lot depth

16 m · 21 m · 23 m · 28 m · 30 m · 32 m · 36 m

Filter Your Perfect Plan

Narrow down our floor plans using the criteria that matter to you:

  • Storey – Single or double storey
  • Beds – Number of bedrooms
  • Baths – Number of bathrooms
  • Ensuite – Master with ensuite
  • Living rooms – Formal and informal living
  • Car spaces – Garage and parking

Facade Options

Choose the look that suits your street and taste. We offer a variety of facade styles:

  • Gable Roof Facade
  • Flat Roof Facade

  • Skillion Roof Facade
  • Mansard Roof Facade

  • Combination Roof Facade

Single-Storey House Plans

Our single-storey designs offer open, easy-to-navigate layouts that suit all ages and lifestyles, with strong connections between indoor and outdoor living. You can see the quality and finish of our work at the following display homes:

  • 23 Egier St, Clyde North
  • 36 Rica St
  • 7 Operatta

Visit our display homes to walk through the layouts and discuss your project with our team. You can also browse our full range of single-storey floor plans online and filter by lot size, beds, baths, and more.

Double-Storey House Plans

Our double-storey house plans provide more living space on the same land footprint by building upwards. Homeowners gain additional rooms and enhanced views, often with separate zones for living and sleeping areas—ideal for growing families or anyone who wants more space without a larger block.

You can filter our double-storey designs by the same lot widths and depths, and by beds, baths, living rooms, car spaces, and facade. Browse the collection below to find a layout that suits your block and lifestyle.
