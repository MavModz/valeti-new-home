Services

  • Home
  • Services

01

Single House Design

Single-storey house plans offer open, easy-to-navigate layouts. Ideal for all ages, they provide flexible living spaces, strong indoor-outdoor connections, and seamless flow between zones.

02

Double House Design

Double-storey house plans provide more living space on the same land footprint by building upwards. Ideal for growing families, they offer separate zones for living and sleeping with enhanced views.

03

Farmhouse Design

Traditional or modern styles that embrace a rural aesthetic. Farmhouse designs feature large verandas, expansive open spaces, and practical layouts ideal for country living.

04

Multi-Unit Development

Multi-unit development creates multiple residential units on a single property through side-by-side or back-to-back construction. Popular for investors maximizing returns on land.

05

Architectural Design

Architectural design creates the plans and specifications for a building. It covers exterior and interior design, site layout, and landscape integration for custom homes.

06

Split Level Homes

Split-level homes combine multiple levels for a dynamic, visually interesting layout. Spacious living areas on the lower level with bedrooms and private spaces above.

07

Uneven Lot

Uneven lot designs are tailored to sloping or irregular terrain. They incorporate terraces, balconies, and outdoor living areas to maximize views and the natural landscape.

08

Knock Down Rebuild

Knock down rebuild involves demolishing an existing building and constructing a new one on the same site. Ideal for staying in your location while gaining a modern home.

09

Corner Lot

Corner lot designs capitalize on corner positions for creative, functional living. They incorporate terraces, balconies, and outdoor areas to maximize light and views.
